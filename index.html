Our Heart & Soul

The Story Behind Kick Back Bakes

“Food shouldn’t just satisfy your hunger; it should bring people together and let the host actually enjoy their own party.”

Kick Back Bakes was founded with one clear mission: to bring the soul of the islands into your home with zero stress. Too often, hosts spend the entire evening running back and forth to the stove, stirring pots, and missing out on the laughter with family and friends.

Chef Tiesha created the "Kick back and we'll cook it" philosophy. From hand-seasoned jerk and slow-cooked oxtail to fresh morning bakes and chilled sorrel, Chef Tiesha brings passion, warm Caribbean hospitality, and transparent pricing ($30/hr) to every single kitchen she enters.

🌿 100% Authentic Flavors

Real herbs, whole spices, and time-honored marinades.

🥖 Baked Fresh Daily

Flaky patties, warm coco bread, and sweet pastries made by hand.

🤝 Stress-Free Hospitality

We prep, cook, serve, and clean up so you can kick back.
